HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our ERP, custom software development, and post-launch support services.

What modules are included in your custom ERP software?
+

Our custom Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) suite includes Core Accounting, Inventory Control, Purchase & Sales Management, Human Resource Management (HRMS), Payroll processing, Customer Relationship Management (CRM), and Distribution logging, all configured exactly to your specific operational flows.

Can your POS system handle multiple retail locations?
+

Yes, our Point-of-Sale (POS) system is designed for multi-tenant and multi-branch synchronization. It features real-time inventory tracking, local offline billing recovery, and a cloud-based central dashboard to monitor performance across all stores.

How long does a typical custom software project take?
+

Project duration varies based on complexity. A custom mobile application or modular system typically takes 8 to 12 weeks, while full enterprise ERP migrations can take 4 to 6 months. We operate on agile sprints to show progress every two weeks.

Do you provide ongoing technical support after deployment?
+

Absolutely. We offer complete SLA-driven post-launch support, including security patching, regular database backups, bug resolution, and feature enhancements. We have flexible weekly and monthly support packages.
