Digital Transformation for Growing SMEs in Pakistan

March 2026 · 6 min read

Digital transformation does not require a full rebuild on day one. Most Softix clients start by digitizing cash flow, inventory, and customer records — then expand modules as teams adopt the system.

Where to begin

Map your pain points: late invoices, stockouts, or multi-branch blind spots. A focused ERP or POS rollout usually pays for itself faster than a sprawling custom build.

Practical next steps

  • Audit current Excel / paper workflows
  • Prioritize finance + inventory first
  • Train cashiers and accountants in short sprints
  • Measure billing cycle time before and after
