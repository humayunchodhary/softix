The ERP Module Checklist Every Growing Business Needs

February 2026 · 5 min read

Choosing ERP features can feel overwhelming. Use this Softix checklist to scope a first release that is useful without being bloated.

  • Finance: ledger, P&L, tax, receivables
  • Inventory: multi-warehouse, batches, reorder alerts
  • Purchase & Sales: POs, invoices, dealer credit
  • HR / Payroll: attendance, leaves, salary slips
  • Reporting: role-based dashboards for owners

Softix ERP is configured around your operational flows — not the other way around.
