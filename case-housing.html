Housing Colony Management Systems

End-to-end society operations for public and private colonies — plot ledgers, automated dues, gate security, and resident self-service.

12+Societies digitized
85%Faster monthly billing cycles
50K+Resident records managed
3×Faster visitor gate processing

Challenge

Societies relied on spreadsheets for plots, dues, and visitor logs — causing billing delays, revenue leakage, and weak gate visibility.

Solution

Softix deployed a modular housing platform covering ownership records, installment plans, maintenance billing, and security pass workflows.

  • Plot registry & ownership transfer workflows
  • Automated maintenance / utility dues
  • Installment books and overdue reminders
  • Gate pass printing & visitor logging
  • Resident portals for requests and notices

Results

Administrators recovered billing cycle time, improved collection visibility, and gave residents a transparent digital channel for services.
